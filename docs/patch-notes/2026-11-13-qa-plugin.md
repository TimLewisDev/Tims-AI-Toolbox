# The qa plugin: bugs and test plans for QA

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Plugins:** qa 0.1.0 (new), core 0.9.1

## Why
The toolbox covered planning and building (engineering) and user stories (design) but nothing for the people who test. QA work comes down to two repeatable jobs: turning "it did something odd" into a bug someone else can reproduce, and breaking a feature into areas to validate and keeping track of what has been tested. Both need a fixed layout (so every bug and plan reads the same) and an honest record (only what the tester said happened).

## Added
- `qa` plugin (needs core): `/plugin install qa@tims-ai-toolbox`.
- `/tt-qa-bug <bug folder> [description]`: a plain-English description becomes a bug (title, severity, environment, numbered steps to reproduce, expected and actual, evidence). It asks only for what the description leaves out and never invents a step. `draft <description file> <spec file>` runs unattended and writes a spec for another tool to review, saving nothing. `scripts/bug.sh` is the only writer of `BUG-NN - <title>.md` (`new`, `edit`, `set`, `show`, `list --tsv`, `validate`, `check`; `--if <hash>` refuses a change to a bug that moved).
- `/tt-qa-test-plan <plan.md> <sources>`: reads a feature's documents and code, proposes areas to validate, and for each area test items (how to test, what a pass looks like), one area at a time for the tester's approval. `draft` writes everything unattended. `scripts/test.sh` is the only writer of the plan and its results (`init`, `area add`, `item add`, `edit`, `remove`, `status`, `check`, `list --tsv`, `summary --tsv`, `verify`).
- `/tt-qa-test-run <plan.md>`: works through the plan an area at a time, records each item's pass, fail, blocked or skip as the tester says, raises a bug from a failing item with its steps and expectation copied across, and reports what is tested, passed and failed.

## Notes
- Results are one row per verdict in `<plan without .md> - Results.tsv`, beside the plan; the last row for an item wins, so a plan can be reworded without losing what was tested. Area and item IDs (`A1`, `A1.2`) are never reused, even after a remove.
- Nothing here talks to an issue tracker. A tool that shows bugs (Tatty's QA mode) files them.
- core 0.9.1: the catalogue and `plugin_hint` know the qa skills.
